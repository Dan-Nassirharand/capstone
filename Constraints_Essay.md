# Project Constraints

Project: GE Appliances, a Haier Company: Agentic Upgrades
Team Members: Dan Nassirharand, Maxwell Blevins

## Security

Developing on the edge of AI agents naturally incurs a security risk due to the volatile
trajectory of the AI industry and associated technologies. This risk, however, does allow for
economic and social advantages in that we anticipate boosts to developer productivity and
both the quality and quantity of user features the organization will deliver. Substantive code reviews,
alignment with security best practices, and testing in isolated environments are strategies we will employ
to manage this risk well. This tradeoff has the potential to be volatile, and naturally demands the most
attention from the team, as an exploited security vulnerability could have disastrous effects on the company's
social, economic, legal, economic, and professional standing.

## Professional

Integrating agentic technology into developer tooling and in backend features allows the
organization an opportunity to familiarize themselves with new technology and brand themselves
as industry-leading professionals. This does, however, come with an economic tradeoff in the
token usage and AI model subscription services required create and maintain agentic workflows.
We hope to minimize this cost through developer education, model selection, and communication
with non-technical stakeholders.

## Social

Using AI agents to boost developer productivity and deliver features to users will improve the lives
of both developers and consumers, respectively. While this comes at the aforementioned economic and
security tradeoffs, the prompt impact we expect to see on developer's and consumer's lives will
serve GE Appliances' professional reputation by confirming they are an organization aligned within themselves
and with their consumers. This is perhaps the most gainful benefit we expect to see from our project,
as satisfied developers and consumers will continue to invest back into the business, potentially remediating the
project's economic tradeoff.
